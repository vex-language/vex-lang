Flat shapes and their areas.

Every shape implements Shape; describe prints one, and Kind tells them apart.

Circle#

struct Circle: Shape

A circle, centred anywhere.

  • The area grows with the square of radius.
  • Its kind is always Kind.round.
let c = Circle(radius: 2.0)
println(c.area())
